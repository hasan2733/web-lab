Ingredients & Steps

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken
  • Saffron
  • Fried onions
  • Fresh herbs

Cooking Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices.
  2. Cook the rice separately until fluffy.
  3. Layer the rice and chicken in a pot.
  4. Top with saffron-infused milk and fried onions.
  5. Slow-cook until everything is well combined.
